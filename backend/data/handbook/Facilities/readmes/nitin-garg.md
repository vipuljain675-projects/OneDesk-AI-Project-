## 👋 Hi, I'm Nitin Garg

I'm a **Systems Engineer** at GitLab. I focus on keeping internal systems like Serval, Decagon reliable, scalable to different teams and easy to use — while constantly looking for ways to automate, simplify, and improve how we work.

Before this, I worked as a senior technical writer, which gives me a deep understanding of the handbook — how it's structured, why it's structured that way, and where it tends to break down. Today, I carry that forward as the technical DRI for handbook content: keeping the documentation accurate, clear, and genuinely useful, and maintaining it as things change.

I enjoy bridging the gap between **systems, support, and automation** — I'm happiest when a manual process turns into a one-click (or no-click) solution.

## What I Do

- Own the reliability and scalability of internal systems like Serval, Decagon end to end.
- Act as technical DRI for handbook content — accuracy, clarity, and upkeep.
- Design and build automations and integrations that remove manual work.
- Diagnose complex issues and drive them to resolution with clear ownership.

## How I Like to Work
